Journey Overview

Discover Nature's Hidden Paradise

Step into a realm where the clouds gracefully descend to embrace lush green hills, and nature's architecture rivals human ingenuity. Meghalaya, the "Abode of Clouds," offers a mesmerizing blend of mist-covered landscapes, crystal-clear rivers, and the vibrant, deeply rooted Khasi culture.

This carefully curated 5-day journey takes you deep into the heart of India's northeast. From navigating the mystical living root bridges to floating on the transparent waters of the Umngot River, every moment is designed to awe and inspire the luxury traveler seeking authentic connection.
