Journey Overview

A Journey Through the Majestic Himalayas

Embark on a breathtaking journey through the heart of Himachal Pradesh, where snow-capped peaks meet vibrant valleys. This luxurious itinerary is designed for discerning travelers seeking an elegant escape into nature. From the colonial heritage of Shimla's Mall Road to the thrilling heights of Rohtang Pass, experience the perfect blend of serene landscapes and curated adventures, ensuring every moment is framed by the grandeur of the Himalayas.

Detailed Itinerary

Transfer from Chandigarh/Delhi, hotel check-in, evening at Mall Road.

Visit Kufri, Jakhu Temple, and Ridge.

Scenic drive via Kullu Valley and Pandoh Dam.

Hadimba Devi Temple, Vashisht Springs, and Tibetan Monastery.

Adventure day with snow activities and breathtaking views.

Visit the hot springs and riverside spots.

Breakfast and transfer for onward journey.
