A Journey to Serenity

Step into a world where majestic snow-capped peaks meet tranquil lakes, and timeless traditions blend seamlessly with modern luxury. The Kashmir Escape is meticulously curated for the discerning traveler seeking solace and splendor in equal measure. Experience the ethereal beauty of Dal Lake at dawn, wander through ancient Mughal gardens, and breathe in the crisp mountain air of the Himalayas.

From exclusive houseboat stays adorned with intricate walnut wood carvings to guided explorations of pristine valleys, every moment is designed to be a cherished memory. Let us guide you through the 'Paradise on Earth' with unparalleled comfort, premium accommodations, and deeply personalized service that reflects the warmth of Kashmiri hospitality.

Detailed Itinerary

Arrive at Srinagar International Airport where our premium representative will warmly welcome you. Transfer to your luxury Houseboat on Dal Lake. In the late afternoon, enjoy a serene 2-hour Shikara ride, watching the sunset reflect off the calm waters, visiting floating gardens and local handicraft markets.

  • check_circle Airport transfer in premium vehicle
  • check_circle Welcome Kahwa on arrival
  • check_circle Dinner at Houseboat

After breakfast, embark on a guided tour of the famous Mughal Gardens: Nishat Bagh (The Garden of Pleasure) and Shalimar Bagh (Abode of Love), built by Emperor Jahangir. Visit the Shankaracharya Temple situated on a hill for panoramic views of Srinagar city. Evening free for leisure or shopping for Pashminas and saffron.

Drive to Gulmarg, a stunning hill station known for its ski resorts and the world's highest green golf course. Experience the thrilling Gondola ride (Phase 1 & 2) up to Mt. Apharwat, offering breathtaking views of the Nanga Parbat. Enjoy a luxury picnic lunch amidst the meadows before returning to Srinagar.

Depart for Pahalgam via Pampore, famous for its saffron fields, and the historic ruins of Avantipur. Check into your luxury riverside resort in Pahalgam. The evening is at leisure to stroll along the banks of the Lidder River and absorb the pristine natural beauty.

Spend the day exploring the stunning valleys of Pahalgam. Visit Aru Valley, a scenic meadow offering a peaceful retreat, and Betaab Valley, known for its lush green terrain and crystal-clear streams. Return to the resort for an exclusive Kashmiri Wazwan dinner experience.

Enjoy a relaxed breakfast at the resort. Later, transfer to Srinagar International Airport for your onward journey, carrying beautiful memories of the Kashmir Escape.
