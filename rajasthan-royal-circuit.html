Rajasthan Royal Circuit

schedule 8 Days / 7 Nights

Experience the Majesty of Rajasthan

Step into a land where history is etched into every stone and every sunset tells a story of valor and romance. The Rajasthan Royal Circuit is a meticulously crafted odyssey through India's most vibrant state, designed for those who seek the pinnacle of heritage and luxury. From the pink-hued corridors of Jaipur to the golden sands of Jaisalmer, experience a world of majestic forts, shimmering lakes, and opulent palaces.

Wander through the living museums of Udaipur, witness the blue-washed charm of Jodhpur, and lose yourself in the rhythmic folk melodies of the desert. Every stay is an invitation to live like royalty, with heritage properties that blend timeless architecture with modern sophistication. Let Adrayah Travels guide you through this royal tapestry with unparalleled service and deep local expertise.

Detailed Itinerary

Welcome to the Pink City. Upon arrival at Jaipur International Airport, our representative will escort you to your heritage hotel. Spend the evening at Chokhi Dhani for an authentic introduction to Rajasthani culture and cuisine.

A full day exploring Jaipur's wonders: Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, and Jantar Mantar. Evening free for shopping in the vibrant Johari Bazaar.

Drive to the Blue City of Jodhpur. Check into your boutique heritage stay and enjoy a relaxed evening overlooking the illuminated Mehrangarh Fort.

Visit Mehrangarh Fort, Jaswant Thada, and the bustling Clock Tower market. Experience the blue-washed lanes of the old city with a guided walk.

Journey into the heart of the Thar Desert. Arrive in Jaisalmer, the Golden City, and witness the fort glowing at sunset.

Explore Jaisalmer Fort and Patwon ki Haveli. Later, head to Sam Sand Dunes for a camel safari and an overnight stay in a luxury desert camp.

A scenic drive to Udaipur, the City of Lakes. En route, visit the stunning Ranakpur Jain Temple. Check into your lake-view resort.

Visit City Palace and Saheliyon-ki-Bari. Enjoy a final boat ride on Lake Pichola before transferring to the airport for your departure.
