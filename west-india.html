Jewels of the West

A 7-Day sample itinerary showcasing the best of Goa.

01

Arrival in North Goa & Sunset at Ashwem

expand_more

Upon your arrival at Mopa International Airport, our premium representative will warmly welcome you. Enjoy a private luxury transfer to your beachfront resort. Spend your first evening strolling along the pristine sands of Ashwem Beach, followed by a curated sundowner dinner featuring fresh local seafood and fine wines.

02

Heritage of Old Goa

expand_more

Step back in time as you explore the architectural marvels of Old Goa. Visit the Basilica of Bom Jesus, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and the majestic Se Cathedral. Our expert historian will guide you through the intricate Portuguese-Manueline styles and share stories of Goa's colonial past.

03

Spice Plantations & Fontainhas

expand_more

Immerse your senses in the lush greenery of a private spice plantation. Learn about the cultivation of cardamom, peppercorns, and vanilla before enjoying an authentic traditional lunch. In the afternoon, wander through the vibrant, narrow lanes of Fontainhas, the Latin Quarter of Panjim, known for its colorful heritage houses and quaint art galleries.

04

Journey to South Goa

expand_more

Transition from the vibrant energy of the North to the serene, untouched beauty of South Goa. Check into a premium beachfront resort tucked away in a coconut grove near Palolem. The afternoon is yours to enjoy at leisure, perhaps with a private yoga session or a spa treatment overlooking the Arabian Sea.

05

Coastal Exploration & Dolphin Spotting

expand_more

Set sail on a private boat for a morning of coastal discovery. Explore the secluded Butterfly Beach and Honeymoon Island, accessible only by water. Keep your eyes peeled for playful dolphins in their natural habitat. Enjoy a gourmet picnic lunch on a hidden cove before returning to your resort.

06

Backwater Serenity

expand_more

Discover the "Scotland of the East" side of Goa with a private cruise through the Sal River mangroves. This peaceful excursion offers a glimpse into Goa's rich biodiversity and traditional fishing villages. Savor an authentic Goan lunch prepared by a local family on the banks of the river.

07

Leisure & Departure

expand_more

Enjoy a final, slow morning of relaxation. Savor a late breakfast with views of the ocean before your private luxury transfer takes you to the airport for your onward journey, carrying with you the cherished memories of your Goan escape.
