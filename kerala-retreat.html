South India

Kerala Retreat

God's Own Country

schedule 6 Days / 5 Nights
chat Enquire via Form mail Get Personalized Quote

A Journey Through Emerald Landscapes

Step into a realm where time slows down to the rhythmic swaying of coconut palms. Our curated Kerala Retreat is an invitation to immerse yourself in the unparalleled beauty of 'God's Own Country'. Drift along the tranquil backwaters of Alleppey in a luxurious private houseboat, breathe in the crisp, cardamom-scented air of Munnar's rolling tea plantations, and rejuvenate your senses with authentic Ayurvedic wellness therapies. From the dense, wildlife-rich forests of Thekkady to the golden sands of Kovalam, this journey is meticulously designed to offer a perfect blend of exotic discovery and sophisticated relaxation.

Signature Experiences

Alleppey

Private Houseboat Cruise

Drift through emerald canals on a luxurious traditional Kettuvallam, enjoying freshly prepared local delicacies as the sun sets over the water.

Munnar

Tea Estate Trails

Wander through seemingly endless emerald tea gardens and learn the art of tea tasting in the cool mountain air.

Thekkady

Aromatic Spice Tour

Engage your senses on a guided walk through aromatic plantations of cardamom, pepper, and cinnamon.

Periyar

Wildlife Encounters

Embark on a gentle boat safari across Lake Periyar, keeping watch for wild elephants, bison, and diverse birdlife in their natural habitat.

Wellness

Ayurvedic Rejuvenation

Restore balance to your body and mind with authentic, expert-led Ayurvedic therapies in a tranquil setting.

Your Curated Journey

Upon arrival at Cochin International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by our representative and transferred to your luxury heritage hotel in Fort Kochi. Spend the afternoon exploring the cobbled streets, marveling at the iconic Chinese Fishing Nets, and visiting the historic Mattancherry Palace. In the evening, enjoy a traditional Kathakali dance performance.

hotel Premium Heritage Stay

A scenic drive takes you winding up into the Western Ghats to Munnar. Over the next two days, lose yourself in the endless expanse of tea estates. Visit the Tea Museum, take a guided walk through the plantations, and drive up to Top Station for panoramic views. Enjoy a private high-tea experience overlooking the misty valleys.

landscape High Altitude Resort

Descend slightly to the spice-scented town of Thekkady. Take an insightful tour through a private spice plantation. Later, experience the serene beauty of Periyar National Park with a boat ride on the lake, offering chances to spot wildlife at the water's edge. Evening at leisure to explore local spice markets.

eco Boutique Jungle Lodge

Drive to Alleppey and board your private, luxury Kettuvallam (houseboat) by midday. Cruise through the labyrinth of palm-fringed canals, observing village life along the banks. Your personal chef will prepare exquisite Kerala cuisine on board. Fall asleep to the gentle lapping of water under a starlit sky.

directions_boat Premium Private Houseboat

Enjoy a final breakfast on the houseboat before disembarking. You will be driven back to Kochi airport for your onward journey, carrying memories of an unforgettable Kerala retreat.
