Signature Experiences

Curated encounters designed to immerse you in the authentic soul of the East.

Darjeeling Himalayan Railway

Darjeeling, West Bengal

Experience the charm of the 'Toy Train' as it winds through emerald tea gardens with views of the majestic Kanchenjunga.

Living Root Bridge Trek

Cherrapunji, Meghalaya

Trek through lush rainforests to witness ancient bio-engineering marvels grown from the roots of rubber trees.

Kaziranga Rhino Safari

Kaziranga, Assam

Embark on a wildlife adventure to spot the rare one-horned rhinoceros in the vast grasslands of the Brahmaputra valley.

Tawang Monastery Visit

Tawang, Arunachal Pradesh

Discover spiritual peace at India's largest monastery, perched high in the Himalayas amidst colorful prayer flags.

Dawki River Boating

Dawki, Meghalaya

Glide over the crystal-clear turquoise waters of the Umngot River, where boats appear to float in mid-air.

Konark Sun Temple

Konark, Odisha

Marvel at the architectural brilliance of this 13th-century UNESCO World Heritage site, shaped like a giant stone chariot.

Detailed 7-Day Sikkim Spectacular

A meticulously planned journey through the mystical landscapes and heritage of the Himalayan kingdom.

01

Day 1: Arrival in Gangtok & Sunset at MG Marg

expand_more

Arrive at Bagdogra Airport/NJP Station where our premium representative will warmly welcome you. Transfer to your luxury hotel in Gangtok. In the late afternoon, enjoy a stroll through the vibrant, pedestrian-only MG Marg, taking in the local culture and mountain air.

  • check_circle Airport/Station transfer in premium vehicle
  • check_circle Welcome tea on arrival
  • check_circle Evening walk at MG Marg
02

Day 2: Gangtok Local Sightseeing & Spiritual Discovery

expand_more

Explore the spiritual heart of Sikkim. Visit the iconic Rumtek Monastery, the Enchey Monastery, and the Namgyal Institute of Tibetology. Witness the serene beauty of the Do Drul Chorten Stupa.

  • check_circle Guided monastery tour
  • check_circle Visit to Flower Exhibition Centre
  • check_circle Traditional Sikkimese lunch
03

Day 3: Glacial Wonders: Tsomgo Lake & Baba Mandir

expand_more

Embark on a scenic drive to the turquoise Tsomgo Lake, situated at 12,400 ft. Continue to the legendary Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir, a site of deep local reverence.

  • check_circle Scenic Himalayan drive
  • check_circle Yak ride experience at Tsomgo Lake
  • check_circle Visit to Baba Mandir
04

Day 4: Journey to the North: Gangtok to Lachen

expand_more

Drive through the rugged beauty of North Sikkim. Witness cascading waterfalls like Seven Sister Waterfalls and Naga Falls. Arrive at the serene mountain village of Lachen, the gateway to the high altitudes.

  • check_circle Drive through scenic mountain passes
  • check_circle Stop at Singhik Viewpoint
  • check_circle Overnight stay in a premium mountain lodge
05

Day 5: Sacred Heights: Gurudongmar Lake & Lachung

expand_more

An early morning expedition to the sacred Gurudongmar Lake, one of the highest lakes in the world, surrounded by snow-clad peaks. Later, transfer to the picturesque village of Lachung.

  • check_circle Visit to Gurudongmar Lake (17,800 ft)
  • check_circle Drive through the Cold Desert of Tibet
  • check_circle Evening relaxation in Lachung
06

Day 6: Valley of Flowers: Yumthang & Zero Point

expand_more

Discover the 'Valley of Flowers' at Yumthang, wrapped in snow-capped peaks and vibrant alpine flora. Optional excursion to Zero Point for a pristine snow experience. Descend back to Gangtok in the evening.

  • check_circle Visit to Yumthang Valley
  • check_circle Hot Spring visit
  • check_circle Drive back to Gangtok
07

Day 7: Departure with Memories

expand_more

Enjoy a final mountain breakfast before your transfer to the airport/station with cherished memories of the mystical East.

  • check_circle Departure transfer in premium vehicle
  • check_circle Souvenir gift from Adrayah Travels
